Privacy policy
Responsible person
Karin Hugentobler, Switzerland. For questions about privacy or to exercise your rights, please use the contact form.

No tracking
This website does not use analytics, advertising or social media plugins and sets no cookies. It does not store anything in your browser. The fonts are served from this website itself, not from Google or another provider. There is no newsletter.

Hosting
The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub automatically processes technical access data such as your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and your browser type. This is necessary to deliver the pages and keep them secure. GitHub may process this data in the USA; it participates in the Swiss-U.S. and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Videos
Some project pages embed videos from Vimeo (Vimeo.com Inc., USA) with the «do not track» setting. When you open such a page, your browser connects to Vimeo (so the videos start faster), and Vimeo receives technical data such as your IP address. Details: Vimeo Privacy Policy.

Contact form
If you use the contact form, your name, e-mail address and message are sent to Basin (usebasin.com), a form service that forwards them to me by e-mail. I only use this data to answer your message and delete it when it is no longer needed. Details: Basin Privacy Policy.

External links
Links to other websites (for example Instagram or project partners) only send data to those sites if you click them. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Your rights
Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU GDPR, you can ask for information about your data and for its correction or deletion. You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
