Perspectives in their diversity
My name is Karin Hugentobler.

I'm a graphic designer who is especially enthusiastic about surprising visual messages that make a difference. Whether it's improving a user flow or a UI, an illustration, an animation or an image – static or in motion – as long as the message moves something.

I love helping people understand complexity. The uniqueness of different stories motivates me to design every day.
