Racism in numbers

Illustrations, infographics and animations on racism in Switzerland

At Hahn+Zimmermann for the Federal Service for Combating Racism

  • Infographics
  • Illustration
  • Animation
Monitoring report for the Federal Service for Combating Racism: «Rassismus in der Schweiz – Zahlen, Fakten, Handlungsbedarf». I made illustrations, infographics and animated videos for the web and social media. They make the figures approachable and complement the data visualisations created by the rest of the team at Hahn+Zimmermann.
