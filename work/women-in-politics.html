Women in Politics

Animated data stories about the share of women in politics worldwide

At Hahn+Zimmermann

  • Animation
  • Data visualisation
  • Photos for project documentation
How well are women represented in parliaments around the world? Short animations turn a large international dataset into data stories with bold typography and music. They are part of the project Women in Politics by Hahn+Zimmermann, which also includes an interactive scrollytelling website, a leporello, a poster and a T-shirt.
Awards for the complete project

Swiss Viz Awards 2025 – Silver

Main challenge

Handling a large dataset in animation – and deciding which of the many stories hidden in the data to tell.

Solution

The data was connected dynamically to the animations in Adobe After Effects, so every country and year could be updated without redrawing by hand. From the many possible stories, each film focuses on one clear message and ends with a call to action.
