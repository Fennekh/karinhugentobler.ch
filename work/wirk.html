WiRK

Logo for food projects for a sustainable Winterthur

Freelance commission for WiRK

  • Logo design
WiRK brings together the people behind Winterthur’s markets, bakeries, shops, restaurants and farms, and develops projects with them that strengthen regional food and a sustainable food system.
The logo is inspired by a leaf of Savoy cabbage – «Wirz» in Swiss German – which also helped shape the name: a regional and widely underrated vegetable.
