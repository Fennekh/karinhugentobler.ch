Baobab Amoureux

Logo for handcrafted salt and pepper mills from Madagascar

Freelance commission for Baobab Amoureux

  • Logo design
The team of Baobab Amoureux makes salt and pepper mills by hand in Morondava, Madagascar – their shape is inspired by the famous «Baobab Amoureux», two baobab trees that grow entwined.
The mills won the European Product Design Award 2026.
