«We book your ticket to any station in Europe!» The young start-up SimpleTrain has set itself the goal of simplifying train travel to Europe. More and more people want to travel by train and are becoming enthusiastic «trainsetters».

It all began in 2019 when I was asked to create a logo. As one of the first five team members, I have been part of the project from the very beginning and continue to shape it to this day. As product owner of the back office, I also look at how to improve our internal flows.

With the support of the Migros Pioneer Fund, the project really gained momentum. Since then, I have designed all kinds of things: business cards, wireframes, letterheads, Lottie files, Christmas cards, shirts, postcards, UX designs, maps, Google Ads, reels, websites, a podcast cover, a shop window for the «Wartsaal Wipkingen» and much more.

Recently we won the tender for SBB group travel, so we now handle all group travel for SBB, and there is a lot to set up.

Main challenge

Making offer creation in the back office faster, without risking mistakes in the decisive step: the ticket booking.

Solution

All fields are open and editable at once (you tab through them), trips are split into legs, and connections can be copied with one click. Parts that are already accepted stay greyed out, so nothing gets mixed up.
