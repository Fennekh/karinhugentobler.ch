Swiss Cheese Production

Pictograms showing the world of Swiss cheese

At Hahn+Zimmermann for Switzerland Cheese Marketing

  • Illustration
  • Pictogram design
A new set of more than 30 pictograms for Switzerland Cheese Marketing, made for presentations on cheese making, milk, the cheese market, traditions and more. The style deliberately follows the existing illustrations on the website: reduced black lines combined with colourful areas. Five colours from the corporate identity give the collection its own dynamic and keep combinations of different pictograms varied.
