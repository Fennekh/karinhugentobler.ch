Wasser

Photo series on deforming water with objects

Propädeutikum ZHdK, 2019
«Object/Space» and «Photography»
Lecturers: István Balogh, Cécile Huber
  • Photography
My project aimed to distort water and use photography to capture this moment. In the end, however, the tools that helped me to deform the water (e.g. wire, fabric, aluminium, glass) became protagonists as well.
