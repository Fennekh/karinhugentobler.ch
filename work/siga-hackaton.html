SIGA Hackathon

A measuring app, designed in 24 hours

For SIGA at the SIGArthon, HSLU

HS 2023
SIGArthon, UI/UX Hackathon
HSLU Rotkreuz 
Hackathon for SIGA
Teamwork with Grace Wu, Julia Merz, Melisa Güda
  • Testing
  • Prototyping
  • Stress test
On the 17th and 18th of November my team «PixelPerfext» and I had the chance to take part in our first UI/UX Hackathon in Switzerland, the SIGArthon 2023. We had 24 hours to design and propose our design of a measurement app for the stakeholder SIGA. To start with, we were given precise information about the persona and a proposed user journey. After 24 hours of questioning, testing, wireframing, prototyping and not too much sleep, we presented our app «TwinDo», which can create a digital twin of a window. It was a great experience with a lot of lessons about teamwork, timing and focusing on the most important things.
