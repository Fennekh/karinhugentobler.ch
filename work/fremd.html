Miggis, Schurrmurr, Durcheinander

Illustrations of lesser-known Zurich German words

Propädeutikum ZHdK, 2019
  • Illustration
Miggis (Zurich German), Schurrmurr (colloquial German), Durcheinander (German): one word, three «languages». The illustrations only make sense once you know what the word means.

A mentored project on the topic of «foreign». When even Swiss German words sound foreign. It was fun to rediscover my own mother tongue.
