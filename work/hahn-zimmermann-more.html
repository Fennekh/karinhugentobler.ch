More at Hahn+Zimmermann

A selection of further illustrations, animations and information design

At Hahn+Zimmermann for various clients

  • Illustration
  • Motion
  • Layout
  • Web design
  • Information design
At Hahn+Zimmermann I had the chance to work on many great projects – illustrations, layouts, animations and websites. Among them: graphics for the strategy «öAV 2030» of the Swiss public employment services for SECO, illustrations and animations for the Migros programme «ici. gemeinsam hier.» on intercultural projects, the strategy illustration «Fit for Future» for the University of Bern, infographics for the annual report of the Presencia foundation, which supports children and young people in Medellín, Colombia, a fact sheet on crop rotation areas for the Federal Office for Spatial Development ARE and the website of the Wilhelm Stiftung, a foundation providing affordable housing.
