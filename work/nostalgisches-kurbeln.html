Kurbeln

Tear-off calendar 2022 with the songs of the Swiss hit parade

BA 4. Semester 2021, IVK FHNW Basel
Woodwork Christian Huber
  • Object design
  • Print
  • Layout
The Swiss music chart has been published annually since 1968. By 2022, that adds up to just the right number of years for a weekly tear-off calendar: the hit of each year gets its own paper strip that can be played on a music box.

To listen to a new song from the Swiss music charts every week, tear the paper strip off the calendar. Put it into the music box with the song facing upwards, turn the handle and dive into nostalgia!

A summer project on the topic of «rhythm». Along the way I discovered a huge music box community where people create their own compositions.
