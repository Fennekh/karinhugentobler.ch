Fernzugangsverzeichnis

Collecting and documenting every WLAN name and mobile antenna in my hometown

BA thesis «Das Komische in Daten», 2022
Visual Communication and Digital Spaces, HGK Basel, FHNW
Lecturers: Kambiz Shafei, Dr. phil. Claire Reymond, Ted Davis
  • Data visualisation
  • Website
  • Photography
  • Print
My thesis topic was humour in data. I saw a lot of comical potential in our relationship to the internet. So I started collecting Wi-Fi names and taking photos of mobile phone towers in my hometown, Pfäffikon. Inspired by old telephone directories, I documented 8,228 Wi-Fi names and 15 mobile phone towers in a village of 12,000 inhabitants. I was interested in the ubiquity of the internet and how much we depend on it.
Explore the WLAN names of Pfäffikon →
Data can create contradictions in what we expect, which can sometimes lead to comical situations. This is why I believe that data visualisation can offer viewers a unique perspective on various topics in a humorous way. In my thesis, my aim was to illustrate a dataset in a manner that highlights the humorous aspects.
