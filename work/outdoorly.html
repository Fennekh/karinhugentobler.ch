Prototyping a personal outdoor assistant

Outdoorly, the AI outdoor activity assistant for your pocket. This project was developed as part of a group work as part of the Passerelle 2023 at the HSLU Design Film Art MA Digital Ideation. It enhanced our understanding of the user-centred approach to designing user interfaces and user experiences. After creating personas, moodboards, requirements, user journeys, wireframes and testing the connection with ChatGPT, we finally got to test our prototype with potential users. This was the most interesting part, because we got a different point of view that helped us see things we wouldn't have thought about in our team alone.
