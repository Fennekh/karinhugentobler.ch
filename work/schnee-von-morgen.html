Schnee von Morgen

A book on technical snow

Bachelor 5. Semester, HS 2021,
Visual Communication and Digital Spaces,
HGK Basel, FHNW
Course «Atlas of Critical Zones»
Together with Anna Bürki
Lecturer: Prof. Marion Fink
  • Information design
  • Book design
  • Layout
  • Screen printing
  • Illustration
  • Maps
  • Photography
The snow line in the Alps has risen by about 300 metres, and the winter season is around 37 days shorter than in 1970. Ski resorts rely more and more on technical snow.

Our book looks at this «snow of tomorrow» with information graphics, maps, illustrations, our own photographs and webcam images of ski slopes. The feel of the paper imitates snow, some pages are screen printed and we bound the book ourselves.
